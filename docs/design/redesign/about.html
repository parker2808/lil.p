§02How I work

Three rules I keep

i.

Migrate in slices

Big rewrites fail. I move one module at a time and keep the app shippable.

ii.

Write the conventions down

A STRUCTURE.md and a scaffold command beat a long review thread.

iii.

Never lose the user's edits

Drafts, diff-only saves, version checks and unsaved-change guards.
